Manager Studio

Internal brand identity for managers at PVH (Calvin Klein + Tommy Hilfiger)

Overview

Managers across Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger had no shared playbook for leading their teams. Manager Studio is an internal brand that gives them one home for expectations, support, and growth resources, shaped through a design thinking workshop with PVH.
