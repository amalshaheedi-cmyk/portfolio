Explore 3.0

Search that understands intent

Redesigning search & filtering on the Explore page — process, research and solution

Overview

Search is one of the main ways people find templates on the Adobe Express Explore page, but only about 41% of searches lead to a click, and that rate has barely moved in a year. This project finds the why and makes filters simpler and adds AI refinement, so people get from search to creating faster.

The prompt bar sets the wrong expectation

A search bar promises results. A prompt bar promises generation

If it opens conversational

Jarring Jump
Searching leaves Explore
Why it matters People typing to find templates land somewhere new. Users shouldn’t be surprised where they land
Impact People who came to browse lose their place. Confused users go back and start over

If it opens a results page

Expectation mismatch
The bar promises generation, the page shows existing templates
Why it matters People read a prompt bar as the first step to creation, so existing templates look like AI output
Impact People second-guess what they’re seeing, then rephrase or search again instead of creating
